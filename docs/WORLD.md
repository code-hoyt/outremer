# The Other World (working notes)

Started 2026-11-12. The other world is its own world, not a spiritual layer of ours. Origin-story specifics wait until later.

## Landing and first impression (locked 2026-11-12)
- Where the heroes land is NOT desert. The opening must give players a clear visual switch: from the Crusader-states desert of the real world straight into an obviously different environment, such as green forest.
- Desert can still exist as nodes or biomes on the map.

## Humans of this world (locked 2026-11-12)
- **Origin:** descendants of past crossings. Tears have opened before, possibly very far back in history. The specific crossings are to be decided later.
- **Look:** at the start, local Humans are only very slightly more tan than the heroes. They look a little different but not markedly so.
- **Faith:** a local religion parallels Christianity but has a different name and history. Its symbol is still a cross or an X. They don't know the events of the Bible; God has worked in this world in different ways. Certain things run in parallel (to be detailed later). The Order of the Eighth Day is an order within this faith (confirmed 2026-11-12). Its name comes from some past holy event that happened on an eighth day (event to be decided later).

- **Place among the races (locked 2026-11-12):** latecomers among older peoples. Peri, Ifriti and Cynocephali were here first, and Humans arrived through the crossings. The older races still see Humans as newcomers, and some resent how far they've spread.
- **In battle (locked 2026-11-12):** balanced stats, no movement quirk. Their edge is the widest class list (an exception to DESIGN.md Q13 for Humans).
- **Dress (locked 2026-11-12):** entirely their own look, a fresh fantasy style for this green world with no historical echoes (not medieval-European, not a patchwork of ancient cultures). The cross or X faith symbol can appear.

### Art brief: native Humans (for Guilhem)
- Native to a green, non-desert world; latecomers among older races, descended from ancient crossings (not shown visually).
- Skin only very slightly more tan than the heroes; otherwise look human, a little different but not markedly.
- Clothing entirely their own: a fresh fantasy style built for this world, no Roman/Persian/Byzantine/medieval-European echoes. Should read clearly apart from the heroes' Crusader kit.
- Faith: a cross or X symbol (Eighth Day's faith), under another name.
- In battle: balanced all-rounders, widest class list; no racial quirk to show.

## Race footprint (Cletus 2026-11-12, direction)
- Some cities are mixed, but not every race is everywhere. Some races have a smaller footprint so they feel rare to the player.
- Mixed cities should NOT feel like just 2 or 3 races; they should feel genuinely diverse (corrected by Cletus 2026-11-12).
- Explore: a fifth race that is the rare one, so the four main races can all be common in mixed cities while one race still feels rare (ties to the quest-unlocked fifth race slot in DESIGN.md Q8/Q12).

## Peri (in progress 2026-11-12)
- **Look direction (via Guilhem, on track not locked):** cute but fierce and pretty like FFTA2's Gria; humanoid with normal legs; slim, elegant, elf-like; wings (silk-moth wings and small crown is the leading pick, dragonfly second).
- **In battle (locked):** quick and fragile; wings give a short hop over gaps, not flight. Nimble core classes plus race-only classes.
- **Sex (locked 2026-11-12):** an all-female people. How new Peri come to be is open lore (idea: blossoms or cocoons, fitting the moth look).
- **Hidden people (direction locked 2026-11-12, inspired by Legend of Dragoon's Winglies):** the Peri were once a great power and demon worshippers. Over the centuries the Humans drove them out. Today they are present but hidden, passing as Humans. Peri are trained from childhood in the magical art of making their wings vanish. Most people think of the Peri as history or legend.
- **Faith today (locked 2026-11-12):** most Peri still follow their old demonic religion. A Peri who turns to the light has to leave friends and family behind, echoing the Christian theme of leaving everything to follow Christ. Every Peri recruit is therefore a convert who chose that cost. Peri enemies who follow the old faith are the norm.
- **Naming (locked 2026-11-12):** Peri who follow the old faith are NOT called "Fallen Peri"; they are simply Peri. Instead, Peri call those of their own who have turned to the light "Fallen". (Exception to the DESIGN.md Q14 naming for Peri.)
- **Where they live (locked 2026-11-12):** scattered and disguised among Humans in Human towns. The ruins of their great old cities lie deep in the forests, where the old worship still gathers in secret. These ruins are the destinations of the Peri side-quest tree and lairs for hostile Peri.
- **Story hook:** Dismas's companion is a hidden Peri. Dismas knows her secret and keeps it, because revealing it could get her killed. Some die-hard Peri-haters exist within the Eighth Day. (Identity and reveal timing are to be decided in the story pass. This echoes Dismas's own past on the profane side.)
- **Unlocking the race:** the main story introduces the Peri as a side note or history note. A Peri side-quest tree reveals more and unlocks Peri as recruits. This overrides DESIGN.md Q12 for Peri: they are not recruitable from the start.
- **Hidden look (locked 2026-11-12):** a hidden Peri passes as Human but has a subtle tell, in the spirit of Legend of Dragoon's Winglies' blue hair: a distinctive hair or eye colour that looks like ordinary character design until the player meets more Peri who share it. The tell is **light blue hair** (locked 2026-11-12, a nod to Dragoon). No Human or other race ever has this hair colour.
- **What the wings mean (locked 2026-11-12):** nothing spiritual; they are simply part of being Peri. They are hidden because of persecution, and they grow stronger with experience.
- **Wings unlock through deeds:** a Peri unlocks her wings through what she does in play, as a battle-forged trait, not by default. Stages (locked 2026-11-12): short hop over gaps, then a glide (free descent from height and crossing a gap of a few tiles), then true flight (ignores height and terrain while moving, Gria-style late power). No flight costs for now (Cletus 2026-11-12).

### Art brief: Peri (for Guilhem)
- An all-female people. Cute but fierce and pretty, like FFTA2's Gria. Slim, elegant and elf-like, with normal humanoid legs (no digitigrade).
- **Light blue hair** on every Peri. It's the race's tell, and no other race shares it.
- **Two states to draw:** (1) hidden: passes as a Human, with the wings vanished by magic and only the light blue hair (plus maybe a faint elegance) giving her away; (2) unfurled: wings out. Silk-moth wings and a small crown were the leading look direction; dragonfly was second.
- The wings are simply biological, not holy and not demonic. They grow with experience: hop, then glide, then true flight.
- **Lore context:** once a great power, they were driven out by Humans over centuries. Most still follow an old demonic religion, and our recruits are converts, whom their people call "Fallen". Their old cities are ruins deep in the green forests. Hostile old-faith Peri are enemies too. They are the same people, so draw them with the same anatomy; the difference shows in dress and bearing, not a corrupted body.
- Dismas's companion is a hidden Peri (her identity is to be decided).

## Ifriti
- **Design approach (2026-11-12):** no "Human, but fire". Start from temperament, follow it into physical tells, and then pick defining visuals (the FFTA2 approach: Bangaa, Viera, Moogles).
- **Temperament (locked 2026-11-12):** bound by their word. They never lie, they bargain carefully, and they keep every oath they swear (inspired by djinn folklore about wishes and pacts). This is the defining trait they are known for and live by, but it is culture and character, not magic: nothing supernatural binds them (clarified 2026-11-18).
- **Base look (locked 2026-11-12):** tall and lanky, with amber eyes. **Skin (changed 2026-11-18, Cletus via Guilhem's locked Oath Mage plate):** dark, leathery skin (a Dunmer-like look), replacing the earlier ashen grey-pale. Wrinkled, worn and ancient-looking is a race trait, but they stay dignified, never undead.
- **Signature feature (changed 2026-11-18):** the ancient look itself: dark, leathery, wrinkled skin that reads as old and dignified at sprite size. The earlier oath-brands (glowing lines burned into the skin, one per oath) were dropped as a race trait; the oath concept now lives only in the Oath Mage class.
- **Other ideas considered, not chosen:** lamp-heart, smoke-veil, seal-collars, glass and ash skin, ringed horns, many hands, brazier crown, script-tongues, unfinished bodies. Kept as options for later.
- **Role (leaning, 2026-11-12):** the most magical race.
- **Homeland (locked for now, may change, 2026-11-12):** proud city-states built on lush green oases, like islands in the desert. Natural magic wells up there and keeps them green, and the Ifriti are drawn to those places. The open desert between them is like a sea. On the map, this means sand nodes with bright magical oasis cities.
- **Faith (locked 2026-11-12):** an older covenant with the same God, kept long before Humans arrived, in a strange, ancient form. They see the cross faith as a younger cousin, which brings both respect and friction. City-states that broke the covenant for old pacts can still be enemies.
- **Toward Humans (locked 2026-11-12, lore only, not a mechanic):** elder teachers who see Humans as young in the faith and guide them, sometimes kindly and sometimes with condescension. Some Ifriti see Humans as oath-breakers because of a past betrayal (to be decided). Recruiting them works the normal way, with no pact mechanic.
- **Footprint (locked 2026-11-12):** common. Ifriti scholars, teachers and merchants turn up in most Human cities, and their home is still the oases.
- How they relate to the other races is open for later.

### Ifriti art brief (for Guilhem)
- **Do not draw "Human, but fire."** Let their temperament shape the body: they are bound by their word, never lie and bargain carefully, so they look still, upright, deliberate and watchful.
- **Base look:** tall and lanky, with long limbs and long hands. Their skin is dark and leathery (changed 2026-11-18 from ashen grey-pale), wrinkled and worn so they look ancient, but dignified, never undead. Their eyes are amber. Elegant, mysterious and dignified, like an old scholar or teacher.
- **Signature (changed 2026-11-18):** the ancient, dark, leathery look. No oath-brands or race-wide oath marks. The Oath Mage's paper wards are that class's look only.
- **Dress:** desert-oasis scholars and merchants. Layered, flowing robes suited to heat, with an air of learned authority. Their style is their own, not Arab or any other real culture.
- **Lore context:** they live in city-states on green magical oases that sit like islands in the desert. They keep an older covenant with the same God and act as elder teachers to Humans, though some distrust Humans as oath-breakers. They are the most magical race.
- **Deliverable:** a full-scale concept portrait like Tristan's, with no 60 px version.

## Cynocephali
- **Design approach:** temperament first, then physical tells, then visuals. Avoid "Human, but dog face".
- **Temperament (locked 2026-11-12):** pack-loyal and fierce. Clan and kin come first, they are fiercely protective and blunt, and they keep a strict pecking order.
- **Base body (locked 2026-11-12):** hunch-ruff. Bulky with a forward lean, no neck, a mane fused into the shoulders, and heavy forearms.
- **Face (locked 2026-11-12):** baboon-muzzled, with a long, heavy muzzle and a deep brow sitting low in the mane. This nods to the medieval dog-headed-men legend, which probably came from accounts of baboons. NOT a dog head.
- **Homeland (locked 2026-11-12):** highland crags. Clan-holds are cut into rocky hills and gorges, and each pack holds its own ridge. A few river clans may live in the lowlands.
- **Faith (locked 2026-11-12):** the Christopher clans. Long ago, a giant Cynocephalus carried a holy child across a river and was changed by it (a nod to the dog-headed Saint Christopher of Orthodox icons). Whole packs converted with their leaders, so many clans hold the cross faith fiercely, while others keep the old ways. They convert pack by pack, which contrasts with the Peri, who convert alone.
- **Toward Humans (locked 2026-11-12):** sworn shield-kin. The converted clans see the Order as a fellow pack in the faith and fight beside it, while the old-way clans are suspicious. As demons grow more present, all the races see value in standing together.
- **Footprint (provisional):** uncommon, mostly in their holds, with some mercenaries and guards in the cities.

### Cynocephali art brief (for Guilhem)
- NOT a dog head on a Human body. Let their temperament (pack-loyal, fierce, blunt, with a strict pecking order) shape the body.
- Body: hunch-ruff. Bulky with a forward lean, no neck, a mane fused into the shoulders, and heavy forearms.
- Face: baboon-muzzled, with a long, heavy muzzle and a deep brow sitting low in the mane (a nod to the baboon origin of the dog-headed-men legend).
- Highland clans, with each pack holding a ridge, so their gear is rugged, mountain-made kit. Many clans keep the cross faith fiercely (the Christopher clans), so some wear a cross. Clan rank could show on the body or gear.
- **Deliverable:** a full-scale portrait like Tristan's, with no 60 px version.

## Hoopoe-kin (fourth main race, picked 2026-11-12)
- Small bird-folk with a big fan crest. Pitched as chatty, nosy, natural messengers and scouts. In legend, the hoopoe was Solomon's messenger to the Queen of Sheba.
- The crest is the instantly readable silhouette (the Moogle pom-pom role), but they are distinctly ours.
- Note: the hoopoe's "hoop-hoop" call is close to the Moogles' "kupo", so avoid a verbal catchphrase.
- **Temperament (locked 2026-11-12):** curious couriers. They are nosy and warm, know everyone's business, and carry letters, news and gossip between every city. Physical tells: a crest that flares with every emotion, quick head-tilts, and satchels crammed with scrolls.
- **Body (locked 2026-11-12):** wing-hands. The arms are wings with clawed, feathered fingers at the tips that can hold a quill or a dagger. They can flutter-hop up ledges but can't truly fly (the sky belongs to the Peri). About waist-high to a Human, with real hoopoe colours: a cinnamon body, black-and-white barred wings, and a crest with black tips.
- **Art request:** show a round version and a skinny version side by side.
- **Home and footprint (locked 2026-11-12):** they have no homeland. They nest in eaves, towers and dovecotes in every city, and their courier network is their nation, with relay roosts along every road. They're the most common non-Human race and are seen everywhere.
- **Faith (locked 2026-11-12):** a bit of everything. They're superstitious magpies who wear charms from every faith they've carried letters for. They're friendly to the cross faith but not committed to it.

### Hoopoe-kin art brief (for Guilhem)
- Do not draw a Moogle with feathers. Let their temperament (curious, nosy, warm couriers) shape the body: quick head-tilts, a crest that flares with mood, and forward-leaning eagerness.
- About waist-high to a Human. Their arms are wing-hands, with clawed, feathered fingers at the wingtips that can hold a quill or a dagger. They flutter-hop but can't truly fly.
- Real hoopoe colours: a cinnamon body, black-and-white barred wings, and a big fan crest with black tips, which is their signature silhouette.
- Gear: courier satchels crammed with scrolls, plus jangling charms and trinkets from every faith.
- **Deliverable:** a ROUND version and a SKINNY version side by side, full-scale like Tristan's portrait, with no 60 px version.

## Racial callings (class groundwork, started 2026-11-15)
Before classes, settle each race's natural calling or occupation, one race at a time. Classes follow from these.
- **Humans (locked 2026-11-15):** holy orders and command. They provide the knights, priests and captains, and they organise the other races into armies. Their race classes lean toward leadership and faith (for example the Standard-Bearer). Being leaders gives them a reason to head a mixed army without being stronger.
- **Ifriti (locked 2026-11-15):** oath-mages. Their magic is sworn: each vow grants power with a limit attached (for example "never retreat" or "never strike first"). These are trade-off upgrades built into the class.
- **Cynocephali (locked 2026-11-15):** shield-wall guardians. They are living walls who stand in front of others and take the hits, and their race classes start as protectors and bodyguards. Pack fighters (stronger beside allies) and mountain hunters (trackers, beast-handlers) are kept as future Cynocephali class ideas.
- **Hoopoe-kin (locked 2026-11-15):** scouts and messengers. They are quick, sharp-eyed runners who spot enemies early, carry orders and flutter-hop to high ground, which grows out of their courier network. Charm-wrights (support casters whose charms from every faith really work, a little) and magpie thieves (pickpockets and snoops who steal items and dig up secrets) are kept as future Hoopoe-kin class ideas.
- **Peri (locked 2026-11-15):** div-hunters. The Peri and the divs (Persian demons) are ancient enemies, and in legend the divs caged captured Peri in iron. Their class is built for demon-slaying out of an old grudge: deadly against demons. (The iron weakness was scratched by Cletus on 2026-11-17.) This ties the rare race straight into the growing demon threat. Light-bearers (healers and purifiers), penitents (power earned through redemptive deeds, after the legend of Peri atoning to re-enter paradise), perfumers (scent auras, since Peri live on the scent of flowers), songweavers (battle-singers) and star-readers (seers who manipulate turn order) are kept as future Peri class ideas.
  - **Look reference for a future Peri class (saved 2026-11-17):** Cletus loves the hooded girl in [this Kojima post](https://x.com/HIDEO_KOJIMA_EN/status/2122834298122404239). It's the costume and silhouette we're borrowing: a deep, soft, oversized hood with long, loose hair spilling out and blowing across the face. Take the vibe only, not the actress's likeness. Her hair is blonde, while Peri hair is locked as light blue, so a blonde Peri would need Cletus's sign-off. That could be a class or variant exception, or the light blue could just flow out of the hood. It isn't assigned to a class yet. The reference images are kept off the public repo, on the shared computer at /workspace/outremer-refs/kojima-hood/.
- **Peri: Div-Hunter (picked 2026-11-17).** The basic slayer: a fast striker with a light blade, deadly against demons, using wing hop, glide and dive to reach targets. No iron weakness. Signature action (picked 2026-11-18): **Scent of Paradise**, a strike that leaves a holy fragrance on the target, burning demons each turn and stopping them from healing (Peri live on the scent of flowers). Flowers and lingering scent are the theme of all her attacks. Other signature-move ideas kept for later moves or abilities: Stooping Dive, Glide-Cut, Iron Cage Turned, Old Grudge, Featherfall Ward, Banish, Hunter's Mark, Unmask and Flurry. The rest of the kit is still to be designed. Other Peri class ideas kept for later: Cage-Breaker, Grudge-Bearer, Skyblade and Ward-Archer.

### First race classes
- **Humans: Templar Knight (picked 2026-11-17).** The heart of the Order, Outremer's take on the FFTA2 Paladin: an armoured holy warrior who leans fighter: a front-line sword-and-shield combatant first, with holy power as an edge rather than a caster kit. Its holy power is a holy striker's (locked 2026-11-17): weapon strikes carry holy damage that hits demons and the profane hardest. Healing belongs to a separate, future Hospitaller class, not the Templar. Weapons (locked 2026-11-17): exclusively one-handed swords and shields, with no two-handers, lances or maces. Signature action (locked 2026-11-17): **Smite**, a simple strike with holy damage that does 1.5x against demons and the profane, with no downside. **Last Stand** (picked 2026-11-17) is an equippable passive, not an action: the lower the knight's HP, the harder its holy strikes hit. Its scaling numbers are still to be tuned and will go in src/config.js when it's built. Actions kept (picked 2026-11-18, rough pitches, numbers still open): **Judgement** (a heavier holy strike that also scores the ground, strongest against demons on profane tiles), **Shield Bash of Faith** (shield blow that knocks back a tile and stuns if the target is profane), **Non Nobis** (battle-prayer giving adjacent allies a holy edge on their next attack), **Beauséant** (plant the banner on a tile; allies near it hold steady and resist fear) and **Relic Blade** (press a relic to the sword so the next few strikes count as holy, but the knight can't defend meanwhile). With Smite that is 6 actions. Ideas kept for later: Crusader's Charge, Riposte of the Cross (likely a reaction).
- **Ifriti: Oath Mage (picked 2026-11-17, mechanic reworked same day).** Its defining moves are oaths that are stat buffs, one per stat (for example Attack, Defense, Jump, Speed). Swearing an oath buffs that stat **for the whole team** (locked 2026-11-17), and a **random limitation is then placed on the Oath Mage's whole team**, like the laws in FFTA and FFTA2 (for example "no ranged attacks" or "no items"). If anyone on the team breaks that limit, the oath breaks, and **the penalty mirrors the oath**: an Attack oath breaks into damage to the whole team, a Speed oath breaks into the whole team being slowed, and so on. This replaces the earlier "backlash damage to the mage" idea. **Oath visuals (locked plate 2026-11-18, `/workspace/art-handoff/deliveries/ifriti-concept-v28/`):** the Oath Mage shows its oaths as small vertical paper wards (like ofuda): a gold-cord lattice of wards down one leg, a hip pouch of flat slips, and two small vermilion wards on a gold collar pin. The wards are this class's look only (Cletus, 2026-11-18); the race has no oath marks. Still open: the list of limitations, the numbers (which will go in src/config.js), and whether oaths stack. Duration (locked 2026-11-17): every oath always ends if the Oath Mage falls. On top of that, each oath has a balancing duration matched to the strength of its buff: stronger oaths last a set number of rounds, and milder ones last until the battle ends or the law breaks. Each oath's duration will go in src/config.js. Oath finishers stay open (2026-11-17): besides rounds or battle end, an oath can also end on other conditions, such as a number of kills, a number of deaths or items used. Keep the possibilities open, and don't lock the list. Stacking (locked 2026-11-17): oaths stack, and stacking oaths means stacking laws, so the player chooses how much risk to take. Each oath is either **once per battle** (only one copy can be active, even with several Oath Mages, and it can't be sworn again unless it breaks) or **stackable** (for example, maybe Attack: each Oath Mage can swear it, the team buff stacks, and every copy adds its own random law). Which oaths stack, and any caps, will be set per oath in src/config.js. Single-target oaths (2026-11-17): some powerful oaths (for example auto-resurrection) affect only one unit instead of the team. Each mage can place one, so bringing several Oath Mages lets you cover several units, one each.
- **Cynocephali: Shieldbrother (picked 2026-11-17).** The basic shield-wall guardian. It takes hits for adjacent allies and gets tougher standing shoulder to shoulder with another Shieldbrother, so formations pay off. Kit ideas so far: **Ridge-Warden** is an action ability (plant on a tile and become nearly immovable, holding high ground and chokepoints, but slow to move once dug in). **Christopher** (carry a wounded or stranded ally to safety, across water or up a ledge) and **Pack-Bulwark** (stronger the more Cynocephali are on the team) are equippable passives. **Howler** (a war-cry guardian that draws attention and shakes enemy morale) is kept as its own future Cynocephali class. Weapons (locked 2026-11-17): shield and mace. For now the game has a single standard shield type. Tower shields might come later as separate equipment, but that isn't decided. Signature action (locked 2026-11-17): **Guard**, which picks one ally in range so that every attack on that ally hits the Shieldbrother instead until its next turn. Other move ideas kept for later: Lock Shields, Bonebreaker and Shield Shove.
- **Hoopoe-kin: Roost-Keeper (picked 2026-11-17).** Sets up roosts on tiles, drawn from their relay-roost courier network, and allies can teleport between them. Reworked 2026-11-17 into a **bird whisperer**: the "teleport" is a flock of birds that swoops up a unit and carries it between roosts. **Roosts can be used by enemies too.** The flock is the class's toolkit: it can also be sent at enemies, and it can take a hit for an ally. Moves picked 2026-11-17: **Pecking Swarm** (the flock blankets a few tiles, lightly damaging and blinding enemies so they miss more) and **Murmuration** (a swirl of birds hides one ally, so attacks on them are likelier to miss until their next turn). Ideas kept for later: Snatch, Carrion Call, Messenger Bird, and Flock Size as a resource. The numbers are still open. Other Hoopoe-kin class ideas kept for later: Courier, Lookout, Herald, Gossip, Skirmisher, Pathfinder, Relay, Decoy and Solomon's Messenger. Other Ifriti class ideas kept for later: Covenant-Keeper, Truthsayer, Ember-Sworn and Witness. Whether it carries a built-in limit (such as a Rule it must keep) is still open, so don't lock one in yet. Kits are still to be designed. The other Human race-class ideas from the pitch are Standard-Bearer, Chaplain, Marshal and Turcopole, plus the Hospitaller (the healing-focused order, suggested by Cletus).
