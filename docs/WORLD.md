# The Other World (working notes)

Started 2026-11-12. The other world is its own world, not a spiritual layer of ours. Origin-story specifics wait until later.

## Landing and first impression (locked 2026-11-12)
- Where the heroes land is NOT desert. The opening must give players a clear visual switch: from the Crusader-states desert of the real world straight into an obviously different environment, such as green forest.
- Desert can still exist as nodes or biomes on the map.

## Humans of this world (locked 2026-11-12)
- **Origin:** descendants of past crossings. Tears have opened before, possibly very far back in history. The specific crossings are to be decided later.
- **Look:** at the start, local Humans are only very slightly more tan than the heroes. They look a little different but not markedly so.
- **Faith:** a local religion parallels Christianity but has a different name and history. Its symbol is still a cross or an X. They don't know the events of the Bible; God has worked in this world in different ways. Certain things run in parallel (to be detailed later). The Order of the Eighth Day is an order within this faith (confirmed 2026-11-12). Its name comes from some past holy event that happened on an eighth day (event to be decided later).

- **Place among the races (locked 2026-11-12):** latecomers among older peoples. Peri, Ifriti and Cynocephali were here first, and Humans arrived through the crossings. The older races still see Humans as newcomers, and some resent how far they've spread.
- **In battle (locked 2026-11-12):** balanced stats, no movement quirk. Their edge is the widest class list (an exception to DESIGN.md Q13 for Humans).
- **Dress (locked 2026-11-12):** entirely their own look, a fresh fantasy style for this green world with no historical echoes (not medieval-European, not a patchwork of ancient cultures). The cross or X faith symbol can appear.

### Art brief: native Humans (for Guilhem)
- Native to a green, non-desert world; latecomers among older races, descended from ancient crossings (not shown visually).
- Skin only very slightly more tan than the heroes; otherwise look human, a little different but not markedly.
- Clothing entirely their own: a fresh fantasy style built for this world, no Roman/Persian/Byzantine/medieval-European echoes. Should read clearly apart from the heroes' Crusader kit.
- Faith: a cross or X symbol (Eighth Day's faith), under another name.
- In battle: balanced all-rounders, widest class list; no racial quirk to show.

- Explore: a fifth race that is the rare one, so the four main races can all be common in mixed cities while one race still feels rare (ties to the quest-unlocked fifth race slot in DESIGN.md Q8/Q12).

## Peri (in progress 2026-11-12)
- **Look direction (via Guilhem, on track not locked):** cute but fierce and pretty like FFTA2's Gria; humanoid with normal legs; slim, elegant, elf-like; wings (silk-moth wings and small crown is the leading pick, dragonfly second).
- **In battle (locked):** quick and fragile; wings give a short hop over gaps, not flight. Nimble core classes plus race-only classes.
- **Sex (locked 2026-11-12):** an all-female people. How new Peri come to be is open lore (idea: blossoms or cocoons, fitting the moth look).
- **Hidden people (direction locked 2026-11-12, inspired by Legend of Dragoon's Winglies):** the Peri were once a great power and demon worshippers. Over the centuries the Humans drove them out. Today they are present but hidden, passing as Humans. Peri are trained from childhood in the magical art of making their wings vanish. Most people think of the Peri as history or legend.
- **Faith today (locked 2026-11-12):** most Peri still follow their old demonic religion. A Peri who turns to the light has to leave friends and family behind, echoing the Christian theme of leaving everything to follow Christ. Every Peri recruit is therefore a convert who chose that cost. Fallen Peri enemies are the norm, not the exception.
- **Story hook:** Dismas's companion is a hidden Peri. Dismas knows her secret and keeps it, because revealing it could get her killed. Some die-hard Peri-haters exist within the Eighth Day. (Identity and reveal timing are to be decided in the story pass. This echoes Dismas's own past on the profane side.)
- **Unlocking the race:** the main story introduces the Peri as a side note or history note. A Peri side-quest tree reveals more and unlocks Peri as recruits. This overrides DESIGN.md Q12 for Peri: they are not recruitable from the start.
- **What the wings mean (locked 2026-11-12):** nothing spiritual; they are simply part of being Peri. They are hidden because of persecution, and they grow stronger with experience.
- **Wings unlock through deeds:** a Peri unlocks her wings through what she does in play, as a battle-forged trait, not by default. Stages (locked 2026-11-12): short hop over gaps, then a glide (free descent from height and crossing a gap of a few tiles), then true flight (ignores height and terrain while moving, Gria-style late power). No flight costs for now (Cletus 2026-11-12).
