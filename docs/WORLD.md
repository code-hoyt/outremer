# The Other World (working notes)

Started 2026-11-12. The other world is its own world, not a spiritual layer of ours. Origin-story specifics wait until later.

## Landing and first impression (locked 2026-11-12)
- Where the heroes land is NOT desert. The opening must give players a clear visual switch: from the Crusader-states desert of the real world straight into an obviously different environment, such as green forest.
- Desert can still exist as nodes or biomes on the map.

## Humans of this world (locked 2026-11-12)
- **Origin:** descendants of past crossings. Tears have opened before, possibly very far back in history. The specific crossings are to be decided later.
- **Look:** at the start, local Humans are only very slightly more tan than the heroes. They look a little different but not markedly so.
- **Faith:** a local religion parallels Christianity but has a different name and history. Its symbol is still a cross or an X. They don't know the events of the Bible; God has worked in this world in different ways. Certain things run in parallel (to be detailed later). The Order of the Eighth Day is an order within this faith (confirmed 2026-11-12). Its name comes from some past holy event that happened on an eighth day (event to be decided later).

- **Place among the races (locked 2026-11-12):** latecomers among older peoples. Peri, Ifriti and Cynocephali were here first, and Humans arrived through the crossings. The older races still see Humans as newcomers, and some resent how far they've spread.

## Race footprint (Cletus 2026-11-12, direction)
- Some cities are mixed, but not every race is everywhere. Some races have a smaller footprint so they feel rare to the player.
- Mixed cities should feel like 2 or 3 races, not all of them.
- Explore: a fifth race that is the rare one (ties to the quest-unlocked fifth race slot in DESIGN.md Q8/Q12). Which existing races are common vs rare is to be decided as each race is fleshed out.
