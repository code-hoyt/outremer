# FFTA and FFTA2 quest catalog

*Research report, 2026-11-24. Page: [FFTA and FFTA2 quest catalog](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-77423492-0240-4ade-b23c-c31d2a9643f0/73629e21-6711-41bd-a9fe-425ff2cc9730). Status: pending review. This is the expanded catalog; it replaces the opening-area-only draft filed earlier the same day.*

A representative catalog of story and side jobs from FFTA and FFTA2, built to help shape Outremer's writ-board. It keeps the opening-area findings used for Chapter 1, then samples mid- and late-game variety: dispatch, escorts, chains, expiry, laws, clan objectives, and unusual win conditions. It is not a complete list of all 300 quests in each game. Nothing here is locked for Outremer.

## Scope and method
FFTA2 entries come mainly from Dev's GameFAQs walkthrough (all 300 pub quests, plus the story walkthrough). FFTA entries come mainly from Terence Fergusson's Quests Guide (all 300 missions: appearance, timeout, clear type, rewards) and KeyBlade999's FAQ/Walkthrough (mission type, availability, location, foes, and story notes). Supporting pages from the opening-area pass (GameSpot, me_frog, Neoseeker, Thonky) are kept where they already supplied posters or early details.

Facts I could not confirm, or where sources disagree, are marked unverified. I did not invent posters, win lines, rewards, or unlocks. Some mid and late FFTA2 poster and reward lines were not recopied from Dev's dense quest-list blocks; those cells say so instead of guessing. The Two Grimoires and From the Rift are story battles in Dev's walkthrough that do not appear in his 300-quest pub list.

Phase is a working label for Outremer, not an in-game term:
- Early: FFTA2 Targ Wood and the first Camoa batch; FFTA's first region through The Cheetahs and the sides that open with it.
- Mid: from the first departure (Wanted: Ugohr / Desert Peril) through the mid-story cluster (Seeking the Stone / Scouring Time).
- Late: from Wanted: Sky Pirate Vaan / The Big Find through the finales and post-game chains.

This catalog has 57 FFTA2 jobs and 46 FFTA jobs.

## What the openings teach
These opening-area findings are unchanged:
- The first fight is off the board in both games (Stranger in the Woods, the Cyril Bangaa duel). The board itself opens with one story job: A Paw Full of Feathers, or Herb Picking.
- A small handful of optionals appears beside the next story fight, not a full list. In FFTA2, Dev notes a couple of optional quests next to The Yellow Wings (The First Step and Green Dominion are in that window). In FFTA, Herb Picking unlocks Thesis Hunt plus a few sides, including the Snow in Lutia follow-up chain.
- The board fills after the opening area opens. Clearing The Yellow Wings and reaching Camoa posts a varied first batch (gathering, traps, a haunted garden, a mark hunt, a delivery). After Thesis Hunt, GameSpot says the FFTA list becomes incredibly long, and rival clans start appearing on the world map.
- Expiry is already in the first hours. FFTA2 sides in this set usually carry a 20-day timer in Dev's list (story jobs are N/A). FFTA sides often timeout in 7 to 20 days, and early Wanted! jobs are month-gated. Outremer's choice to keep all six Chapter 1 writs unmissable is a gentler on-ramp than the games it is copying.
- Small jobs are fun because the win condition changes, not because the lore is deep. Reagent Run and Beetle in a Haystack are reach-and-examine. Trappings of Failure is destroy the traps. Cyanwolf is kill the mark, not the pack. Foodstuffs: Texture is procure ingredients. Snow in Lutia is a short, ordinary fight that unlocks two follow-ups.
- Follow-ups in the opening are light and often just an item handoff. Trappings of Failure feeds The Perfect Gift; Reagent Run can complete The First Step; Snow in Lutia unlocks Frosty Mage and Prof in Trouble. Longer chains, like the Star Seal, are posted early but paid off much later.
- Clan-versus-clan shows up early and clearly. Thesis Hunt and The Yellow Wings are both "drive off another company." The Camoa Cup even brings the Yellow Wings back, but it is rank 19 and month-gated, so it is a first-batch posting, not a first-hour fight.
- Protecting a non-combatant, or catching foes before they flee the map edge, does not appear in these opening sets. Those are fine lessons, just not ones either game teaches in the first region.

Two opening details the fuller pass adds, without changing that Chapter 1 read:
- Dispatch-only writs can appear in FFTA2's first Camoa batch, but only in Goldsun and only for ten days (Wanted: Barmaid!, Great Land Festival). They are easy to miss, so they are not a reliable first-hour lesson.
- Green Dominion and Foodstuffs: Texture also post in that early window, adding a rumor fight and an ingredient hunt beside the jobs already logged.

## Chains
- FFTA2 likes long, shape-shifting chains. Mack's writs start as a cactus-fruit delivery (The First Step), become a mid-game medicine run (The Next Step), turn into an escort (A Step Further), and end as another quiet item swap (The Last Step) after A Request. The seal chain is posted in Targ Wood and paid off much later. Foodstuffs and the Bonga Bugle run across months. Death March is three Uphold the Law fights. Cake: The Recipe gathers pages, then later writs cook the payoff. Yellow Wings in Trouble is a late callback that makes the opening bandits the people you protect.
- FFTA likes rumor-gated chains and parallel sets. Snow in Lutia seeds two follow-ups in the first region. Free [town] writs and the four sigils are spread across the mid game. Foreign Fiend is three identically named fights plus Last Stand. The Dark, Hero, and Fey Blades are parallel post-game hunts, not one ordered ladder.
- Notice and rumor gates matter more later. FFTA2 often asks you to read a pub notice (Medicinal Marvel, the Bonga Bugle, What's Really Important) before the next link appears. FFTA uses rumors the same way (Area Freed!, Thief Exposed, Foreign Fiends, The Sages).
- For Outremer: keep Chapter 1 to one short follow-up, as already locked. From Chapter 2, a chain can change type (delivery into escort) and can wait on a writ-board notice. Save callbacks (a crew you already fought, now asking for help) for later chapters.

## Expiry design
- Story stays unmissable in both games (FFTA2 Days: N/A; FFTA Timeout: ---). Sides are where the clock runs.
- FFTA2's default side timer is 20 days, but it is not uniform. Cups are often 10 days. Dispatch festival jobs in the first Camoa batch are 10 days and month-gated. Wayfarer Meet Objectives can be 5 or 6 days. Airship-cleaning writs are 4 days and must be picked up in the right town. The Bonga Bugle series uses 3- to 8-day limits, the tightest regular timers in Dev's list.
- FFTA uses three clocks at once: a timeout (often 7, 10, or 20 days), sometimes a short availability window (10 days in Cyril for Fire! Fire!, 15 or 25 days at a listed pub), and sometimes a month gate (early Wanted! jobs). Town-liberation Captures and many area-hunt dispatches timeout in only 5 days. Some Encounters are "25 days only" or "35 days only."
- For Outremer: the Chapter 1 rule (no expiring writs; expiry from Chapter 2) still matches the sources. When expiry arrives, copy the later-game pattern, not a single timer: keep story and key chain links unmissable, use a default side window, and reserve short timers for special paper (festival, monthly news, a wayfarer's expedition). Month-gated festival dispatch is a Chapter 2 flavor, not a Chapter 1 tutorial.

## Dispatch
- FFTA is mostly dispatch. Terence's clear field marks roughly 170 of 300 missions as [Dpatch], with durations in days, battles, or enemies defeated. KeyBlade labels 164 Dispatch and 24 Capture/Dispatch. A unit leaves the party, success is rolled from a hidden difficulty versus the unit's stats, and recommended jobs and forbidden jobs can swing the odds (Thonky's dispatch guide).
- FFTA2 uses dispatch much more sparingly as a required win. Dev lists only about a dozen pub quests whose victory line is "Dispatch the right person for the job." Many other fights list a Recommended Job (Dispatch), which is an optional send, not the quest type. FFTA2 also has 30 Delivery writs that are item handoffs or travel, with no fight described.
- Dispatch can appear early, but not as the first lesson. FFTA's Watching You posts after The Cheetahs; Free Cyril is a 3-day liberation dispatch. FFTA2's Barmaid and Great Land Festival post in the first Camoa batch only during Goldsun.
- For Outremer: do not make dispatch a Chapter 1 writ-board lesson. From Chapter 2, a minority of everyday writs can be "send a man" jobs, closer to FFTA2's rare dispatch-only postings than to FFTA's dispatch-heavy board, unless we later decide the board should feel as busy as FFTA's.

## Variety of win conditions
- After the opening, "defeat all foes" is no longer the default. FFTA2's 300 pub quests include, in Dev's types, 91 Satisfy Petitioner, 48 Battle, 30 Delivery, 25 Defeat Mark, 22 Escort, 20 Story Battle, 20 Investigation, 18 Meet Objectives, 12 Headhunt, 11 Item Recovery, and 3 Uphold the Law. Wins include protect a charge, examine then warp, gather pages or gemstones, clean an airship in four rounds, visit three forests in six days, stamp a wayfarer's log, donate gil, recruit a member, uphold the law, and speed-battle House Bowen.
- Escorts are common in mid and late FFTA2 (22 of them) and show up in late story too (defend the sky pirates in Wanted: Sky Pirate Vaan). They are still absent from the first-region fights.
- Catching foes before they flee a map edge was not found in Dev, Terence, or KeyBlade. Some bosses flee at low HP (Klesta; a later beast you then hit with Cloudy Sap; a Night Dancer who flees when defeated). That is not the same rule as a map-edge escape timer.
- FFTA's variety is as much about job type as about the battlefield: Engagement, Dispatch, Capture (town liberation), Encounter (sometimes a short window), and multi-part story fights (Quiet Sands and Present Day are two-part; Royal Valley is three-part). The Bounty makes you the mark. Jagd Hunt removes the Judge: a still-KO ally is dead.
- Laws stay important, but they change role. FFTA laws can jail a unit or, in a Jagd, cost a life. FFTA2 laws revoke revival and bonuses, and a few mid-late writs (Death March) make upholding the law the objective. Clan objectives in FFTA2 also include Meet Objectives travel, timed chores, and clan-talent gates (Negotiation, Teamwork, Adaptability).
- For Outremer later chapters: add one new win rule at a time (escort, dispatch, timed travel, uphold a rule, protect a returning crew). Save multi-part story fights and no-judge or permadeath experiments for late acts if we want them at all.

| The Castellan's Toll | Keep | A rival company at a ford is exactly what Thesis Hunt and The Yellow Wings already do in hour one. |
| Lost Sheep | Keep | Reach points on the map is a direct echo of Reagent Run and Beetle in a Haystack. |
| What the Thieves Left Behind | Keep | One follow-up is right. Unlock it when Tithe Thieves is cleared, even if that is before the patrol wave, the way Snow in Lutia immediately seeds its follow-ups. |

Suggested swaps, at most two, still: replace the escort with a marked-beast hunt, and drop the flee-the-edge rule from Tithe Thieves so it is a normal bandit fight. The follow-up can stay. If the escort is worth keeping as a unique Chapter 1 tutorial, keep it and only make the Tithe Thieves tweak, but plan more escorts for later chapters either way.
