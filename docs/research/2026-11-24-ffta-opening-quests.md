# FFTA and FFTA2 opening quests

*Research report, 2026-11-24. Page: [FFTA and FFTA2 opening quests](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-77423492-0240-4ade-b23c-c31d2a9643f0/73629e21-6711-41bd-a9fe-425ff2cc9730). Status: pending review.*

A compact catalog of story and side jobs from FFTA2's Targ Wood and first Camoa batch, and from FFTA's first region, to help shape Outremer's six Chapter 1 writs. Nothing here is locked for Outremer.

## Scope
This covers FFTA2's Targ Wood story line through The Yellow Wings, plus the first batch of jobs that appear after you reach Camoa, and FFTA's first region: the Cyril tutorial, Herb Picking, the small handful of sides that open with it, Thesis Hunt, and the first jobs that appear beside it. Later follow-ups such as The Next Step, the Moon and Sun Seals, and Wanted: Ugohr are named only when an opening job points to them.

Facts come from Dev's FFTA2 GameFAQs walkthrough, Terence's FFTA quest guide, and supporting pages on GameSpot, me_frog's GameFAQs walkthrough, and Neoseeker. Where those sources disagree, or a poster or win line was not in the pages checked, the row is marked unverified.

## What the openings teach
- The first fight is off the board in both games (Stranger in the Woods, the Cyril Bangaa duel). The board itself opens with one story job: A Paw Full of Feathers, or Herb Picking.
- A small handful of optionals appears beside the next story fight, not a full list. In FFTA2, Dev notes a couple of optional quests next to The Yellow Wings (The First Step is one). In FFTA, Herb Picking unlocks Thesis Hunt plus a few sides, including the Snow in Lutia follow-up chain.
- The board fills after the opening area opens. Clearing The Yellow Wings and reaching Camoa posts a varied first batch (gathering, traps, a haunted garden, a mark hunt, a delivery). After Thesis Hunt, GameSpot says the FFTA list becomes incredibly long, and rival clans start appearing on the world map.
- Expiry is already in the first hours. FFTA2 sides in this set carry a 20-day timer in Dev's list (story jobs are N/A). FFTA sides often timeout in 7 to 20 days, and early Wanted! jobs are month-gated. Outremer's choice to keep all six Chapter 1 writs unmissable is a gentler on-ramp than the games it is copying.
- Small jobs are fun because the win condition changes, not because the lore is deep. Reagent Run is reach-and-examine. Trappings of Failure is destroy the traps. Cyanwolf is kill the mark, not the pack. Snow in Lutia is a short, ordinary fight that unlocks two follow-ups. Posters are towns, merchants, a wildlife office, an apothecary league, a greengrocer.
- Follow-ups in the opening are light and often just an item handoff. Trappings of Failure feeds The Perfect Gift; Reagent Run can complete The First Step; Snow in Lutia unlocks Frosty Mage and Prof in Trouble. Longer chains, like the Star Seal, are posted early but paid off much later.
- Clan-versus-clan shows up early and clearly. Thesis Hunt and The Yellow Wings are both "drive off another company." The Camoa Cup even brings the Yellow Wings back, but it is rank 19 and month-gated, so it is a first-batch posting, not a first-hour fight.
- Protecting a non-combatant, or catching foes before they flee the map edge, does not appear in these opening sets. Those are fine lessons, just not ones either game teaches in the first region.

## Check of the pitched six
The pitch is: Wolves on the Mill Road first; The Salt Merchant's Escort and Tithe Thieves after the first story battle; The Castellan's Toll, Lost Sheep, and What the Thieves Left Behind when the patrol quest opens. Everyday writs, exactly one follow-up, none expire. That pacing is sound. Two small swaps would bring the jobs closer to what these openings actually do.

| Writ | Fit | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Wolves on the Mill Road | Keep | Closest to A Paw Full of Feathers and to Herb Picking's animal fight. A clean first board job. |
| The Salt Merchant's Escort | Swap | Neither opening teaches protect-a-non-combatant. A marked-beast hunt in the Cyanwolf or early Wanted! mold would teach a new win rule these games do use. |
| Tithe Thieves | Tweak | A bandit job belongs here, like Thesis Hunt or The Yellow Wings. Catching them before they flee the map edge does not show up in these openings; a straight defeat-the-thieves fight is closer. |
| The Castellan's Toll | Keep | A rival company at a ford is exactly what Thesis Hunt and The Yellow Wings already do in hour one. |
| Lost Sheep | Keep | Reach points on the map is a direct echo of Reagent Run, the first standout FFTA2 side after Camoa opens. |
| What the Thieves Left Behind | Keep | One follow-up is right. Unlock it when Tithe Thieves is cleared, even if that is before the patrol wave, the way Snow in Lutia immediately seeds its follow-ups. |

Suggested swaps, at most two: replace the escort with a marked-beast hunt, and drop the flee-the-edge rule from Tithe Thieves so it is a normal bandit fight. The follow-up can stay. If the escort is worth keeping as a unique tutorial, keep it and only make the Tithe Thieves tweak.
