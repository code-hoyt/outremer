# How FFTA and FFTA2 recruit clan members

*Research report, 2026-11-23. Page: [How FFTA and FFTA2 recruit clan members](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-77423492-0240-4ade-b23c-c31d2a9643f0/04666a12-020e-4b2c-9619-d90488cae2a2). Not locked; see [README.md](README.md) for status.*

> **Status (2026-11-23):** Cletus decided to copy the FFTA2 recruiting system (DESIGN.md "Recruiting: FFTA2-style"). The DESIGN.md conflicts listed below were fixed the same day. The side-character join level (item 3) is an open question in TASKS.md.

A reference for Outremer: exactly how Final Fantasy Tactics Advance (GBA, 2003) and Final Fantasy Tactics A2 (DS, 2007) bring new members into your clan, plus where that clashed with what we'd already locked.

## The short version
Both games use the same idea. Story characters join on their own, a few named secret characters join through optional quest chains, and everyone else is a generic unit with a random race, job and stats who asks to join and can be accepted or turned away. The clan holds 24 members and about six deploy per battle. The big difference is control. FFTA hands you random offers after missions. FFTA2 ties generics to a place and a month on the world map, and adds a quest that lets you pick the job.

## Side-by-side comparison
| Topic | FFTA (GBA) | FFTA2 (DS) |
|---|---|---|
| Where generics come from | A random offer after finishing a mission (not random clan battles) | A popup on the world map when you enter certain areas in certain months |
| What decides race | Mostly random; mission items can tilt it | The calendar month (each race owns two months) |
| What decides job | Random, mostly basic jobs; items can unlock expert jobs | The area's job list, filtered to jobs you've unlocked and story progress |
| Level | Your clan's average level | Your clan's average level (reported to cap at 30) |
| Choosing the job | Only by luck, items or save-reloading | The "Clan Mates" quest: answer four questions to pick the job |
| Guaranteed recruits | "[Race] Wanted" dispatch missions, only while the clan has fewer than 6 members | "Clan Mates" quest (race set by month), repeatable |
| Accept or decline | Yes, for generics | Yes for generics ("turn unit away"); you can see stats, gear and abilities first; story characters can't be refused |
| Clan cap | 24, including Marche and Montblanc; nobody offers to join when full | 24; when full you're offered a swap with an existing member |
| Dismissing | Generics, and secret characters (who can rejoin via repeatable missions) | Generics only; story and secret characters are permanent |
| Secret characters | 5 secret (unique sprites) and 6 semi-secret (generic sprites, named), mostly post-game | 5 secret (Frimelda, Vaan, Penelo, Al-Cid, Montblanc) via quest chains, some post-game |
| Permanent loss | Yes: a unit KO'd and not revived in a Jagd is gone forever | No permadeath found in sources |

## FFTA (GBA)

### Generic recruits
- You start with six: Marche, Montblanc and four generics.
- After most missions there's a small chance a random unit asks to join. Random clan battles on the map don't count.
- Race and job are mostly random and usually basic jobs. Expert jobs (Assassin, Paladin and so on) are rare.
- Mission items you bring along shift the odds. Ally Finder targets basic jobs. Ally Finder 2 and Ogma's Seal open up expert jobs and add about 20% to the join chance.
- Fan lists also tie race to items (for example Esteroth for Humans, Love Potion for Viera) and job type to trophies. These come from a community post, so treat them as likely, not confirmed.
- Whether the month affects race is disputed. One guide says it does; a board regular says months only change which "[Race] Wanted" mission appears.
- New recruits join at your clan's average level, with no abilities learned and no equipment, even if they start in an expert job.

### Guaranteed recruits
If your clan drops below six members, "[Race] Wanted" dispatch missions show up in the pub, one race per month. Clearing one guarantees an offer from that race. Players use this early on to hand-pick a core team, then fill the rest of the clan with low-level units to keep the average level down.

### Secret and semi-secret characters
- **Ezel (Nu Mou, Hermetic):** late game: talk to him at the Cadoan card shop, clear his missions, then "Reconciliation". The only secret character before the ending.
- **Ritz (Human, Viera job set):** post-game mission "Mortal Snow".
- **Shara (Viera, Sniper):** post-game mission "A Maiden's Cry", then visit any town.
- **Babus (Nu Mou, Runeseeker):** post-game "Left Behind", then "With Babus".
- **Cid (Human, Judgemaster):** after clearing all 300 numbered missions; a cutscene at Bervenia leads to a mission where he joins.
- **Semi-secret: Eldena, Lini, Cheney, Pallanza:** bring a specific one-of-a-kind item (Elda's Cup, The Hero Gaol, Snake Shield, Wyrmstone) on any mission; the join is a random roll each time.
- **Semi-secret: Quin, Littlevilli:** may offer to join after "Missing Professor" and "Clan League".

Secret characters always offer once the steps are done, as long as there's room. They arrive with good gear and mastered abilities. Ezel, Babus and Cid can't change jobs. Special recruits join a few levels above the clan average (one source says about +4). If you dismiss or refuse them, repeatable missions let you get them back.

### Losing members
You can dismiss anyone except Marche, and a dismissed generic is gone. Law breakers go to prison for a set number of battles unless you pay bail. In a Jagd (lawless zone), a unit KO'd and not revived by the end of the fight is lost for good.

## FFTA2 (DS)

### Starting clan
Luso starts as a Hume Soldier alongside a Viera White Mage, a Nu Mou Black Mage, a Bangaa Warrior and a Moogle Thief, so you begin with one of each original base race. Seeq and Gria come later. Cid is a guest at first and joins for real later.

### Recruiting on the map
| Race | Months | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| Hume | Blackfrost, Skyfrost | Targ Forest, Galerria Deep, Sant D'alsa Bluff |
| Bangaa | Greenfire, Bloodfire | Baptiste Hills, Aisenfield |
| Nu Mou | Rosefire, Coppersun | Graszton, Moorabelle, Nazan Mines |
| Moogle | Goldsun, Silversun | Bisga Greenlands, Goug, Neslowe Passage |
| Viera | Ashleaf, Mistleaf | Camoa, Rupie Mountains |
| Gria / Seeq | Emberleaf, Plumfrost | Fluorgis, Zedlei Forest / Aldanna Range, Tramdine Fens |

- Enter the right area in the right month and a bubble appears. Walk to it and a recruit asks to join.
- Each area has its own job list. Early areas give basic jobs and later areas give advanced ones.
- You only meet jobs you've already unlocked for your clan, and advanced jobs also need enough story progress.
- Each area gives one recruit per in-game year (240 days).
- Some races are gated: Viera need the "Moon Seal" quest, and Gria need the second continent.
- You see the unit's stats, gear and abilities before you say yes. Stats are rolled when the offer appears.
- Recruiting opens once you reach the world map at Camoa.

### The Clan Mates quest
After the story's auction event, a Rank 1 pub quest called "Clan Mates" opens, and it comes back over time. A Nu Mou in Targ Wood asks four A-or-B questions. The month sets the race and your answers set the job. If that job isn't unlocked yet, he offers a stand-in. You can refuse up to three times before he gives up. This is the one place either game lets you order a specific job.

### Story and secret characters
- **Story: Luso, Cid, Adelle, Hurdy:** join through the main story. Can't be refused, dismissed or dispatched. Only Luso is required in battle.
- **Frimelda:** quest chain: Under the Weather, A Chill in the Night, Show of Strength, Hunted, then Memories Forged, where she joins.
- **Penelo and Vaan:** right before the final quest, events in Fluorgis: Penelo joins first, then Vaan after a battle.
- **Al-Cid:** after Vaan and Penelo and "A Dashing Duel", finish the game, then answer "An Elegant Letter" from a post-game save.
- **Montblanc:** post-game: collect Giga's Pendant, Pin of Order and Earrings of the Dead, then take "Wanted: Friends, Kupo!".

Secret characters are optional because you can skip their quests. Once one joins, though, they're permanent. If you collect all nine named characters, only 15 of your 24 slots are left for generics.

**Moogle Knights:** they aren't special characters in FFTA2. Moogle Knight is a regular Moogle job, so you get one at Goug in Goldsun or Silversun, or through Clan Mates, once the job is unlocked.

### Clan privileges, trust and items
I found nothing showing that generic recruits need clan privileges, trust or items in FFTA2. The only items in recruiting are the three Montblanc needs. One player reported getting more recruit offers after raising the clan's title through clan trials, but the guide author thinks trials don't matter and story progress is what counts. Treat it as unproven.

## What's shaky in the sources
- The level formulas come from players testing, not official data. FFTA2's cap of 30 is labeled "not 100% confirmed" in the main guide.
- FFTA's +4 levels for special recruits comes from one board post. Other guides only say "a few levels above average".
- In FFTA, whether the month affects random race is disputed, and the item-to-race list comes from a reposted community note.
- FFTA2's secret-character quests have different names in different sources. A GameFAQs answer lists "A Refined Recruit", "An Elegant Encounter" and "Where Could He Be?", while the recruitment guide describes event bubbles and "An Elegant Letter". The steps are the same; only the names differ.
- Story characters in FFTA2 seem to join at their own set level rather than the clan average. One player had a level 11 Adelle while the clan was at 45. That's a single report.

## What this means for Outremer
1. Use FFTA2's three tiers. Story heroes (Tristan, Aveline, Godric, Lucien, Dismas) are permanent and can't be refused. Side characters from Chapter 2 on act like FFTA2's secret characters: optional quest chains, permanent once they join. Generics fill the rest of the roster.
2. For generics, use FFTA2's map recruiting: race by region or season, a job list per place, limited to classes you've unlocked, with stats shown before you accept. That gives replays variety and lets players plan. Our rare traits that only roll on random recruits fit right into that offer card.
3. Starting with Dismas, a Cynocephalus and a Hoopoe-kin matches FFTA2's one-of-each start in spirit. When we come back to clan size, adding a Human and an Ifriti hunter would give one of each base race, just like Luso's opening clan.
4. Pick a cap and swap rule early. Both games use 24. FFTA2 offers a swap when you're full, which is friendlier than FFTA silently skipping offers. Count story characters against the cap, and remember permanent members shrink the generic pool (FFTA2 leaves 15).
5. Hiding Peri behind a side-quest tree is a direct match for FFTA2's Viera and Gria gates. A "Clan Mates"-style quest where you pick the class could come back as a side mission from Chapter 2 on without bringing back the dropped recruit missions.
6. Before any of this is built, fix the DESIGN.md lines listed above. *(Done 2026-11-23; Cletus chose the FFTA2 way: all companions gettable in one run, variety from random generics.)*
