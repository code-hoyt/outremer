# Outremer: story bible

The working doc for story decisions, written with Cletus. Earlier test characters (Renaud, Yusuf, Wren) are set aside; the story starts from scratch (2026-11-09).

## Core (locked 2026-11-09)
- **What the player should feel at the end:** satisfied and happy. The heroes win, and the win is earned.
- **Moral frame:** the Christian knights are the good guys. Show it through their deeds (keeping vows when it costs them, protecting the weak, mercy, staying true) rather than telling it.
- **Tone:** a clear, triumphant, heroic story, closer to classic Final Fantasy or Tolkien than to something grim and grey.

## Antagonists (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Both:** a human antagonist early on who turns out to serve a darker power.
- **Demons** are real and play a part.
- **Saints and angels** are on the side of the light, possibly as summons in combat.

## How real the myth is (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Open from the start:** the supernatural is an accepted part of the world (relics, blessings, known legends).
- **Escalation:** the war first looks like a normal one, but strange events build until the true, demonic scale of the threat is revealed.

## Setting (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Lightly fictionalised Crusades:** our own characters and smaller towns and castles in the real Crusader states. Real kings and campaigns are heard about in the background, but the story is our own.
- **Two layers:** the real world, and a spiritual side of the same world where the true (demonic) enemies are seen. The story starts in the real world and crosses over as it escalates.
- **Gameplay idea:** a map's spiritual version could have different terrain, demons in place of soldiers, and saints you can call on.

## Crossing over (locked 2026-11-09)
- **The tear:** a Satanist cult tears the veil, and it pulls the heroes into the other world early in Act 1 (like the book in FFTA).
- **Sceptical protagonist:** he doesn't really believe in the supernatural, then lands in a world where it's everyday and is almost in shock. His arc runs from scepticism to conviction.

## Mechanic idea: holy and profane ground (leaning, 2026-11-09)
- Territory you hold is holy, and enemy territory is profane.
- Map nodes are worth taking for what they offer (shops, mercenaries, upgrades, saint summons). Winning a node consecrates it; profane nodes favour demons.
- No extra button press; it comes out of which battles you choose. Different node priorities make each playthrough different.

## Opening and the scattered squad (locked 2026-11-09)
- **The squad is scattered** when the veil tears. Finding each other is an early goal.
- **Opening scene:** the protagonist is pulled through alongside one of the Satanists. Both arrive confused. A Fiend finds them, the Satanist worships it or panics, and it kills him. That's the hero's crash course in the new world.
- **His squad (about 3, found later in different places):**
  - a loyal friend;
  - a love interest;
  - someone who switches sides (direction to be decided; suggestion: starts on the wrong side and earns redemption).

## Cast so far (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Protagonist:** a young Templar knight. He's sceptical, not bitter: he has never seen anything supernatural himself and has quiet doubts nobody answered. The other world finally gives him something to see.
- **Reunions in order:**
  1. **The medic (love interest):** starts as a cute, kind medic; the relationship grows over the story.
  2. **The loyal friend:** hot-headed, but holds strong when it counts.
  3. **The prodigal son:** from the hero's own side, found having joined the enemy; later returns to the good.

## Names (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Tristan:** the young Templar knight (MC).
- **Aveline:** the medic and love interest.
- **Godric:** the hot-headed loyal friend.
- **Lucien:** the prodigal son. **Aveline's brother**, which makes his betrayal hit harder and his return pay off for both of them.

## Classes and backstories (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Tristan, Templar knight:** a sword and shield frontliner and solid all-rounder. A younger son of minor nobility who took the vows because the family had nothing else to give him. Sincere about the order, quietly unsure about God.
- **Aveline, battlefield medic who grows into a Saint-Caller:** a healer who learns to call on saints in the other world. She served in the Hospital tending wounded soldiers and is the one in her family who always holds everything together.
- **Godric, heavy hitter:** a big axe or mace, high damage and charges over defence. An English or Saxon-born common sergeant, quick to anger and fiercely loyal to Tristan, who once stood up for him.
- **Lucien, skirmisher and scout:** fast and clever, with a bow or twin blades. Aveline's younger brother, bright and ambitious, resentful of being overlooked; the cult uses that resentment to turn him.

## Story beats to keep
- **Aveline breaks:** a moment where she can no longer hold things together (likely on seeing Lucien with the enemy), and Tristan steps in. A natural midpoint for both his faith arc and the romance.

## Villains (locked 2026-11-09)
- **The Lord:** a charming, well-liked baron in the real world, secretly working for the cult. Lucien looks up to him.
- **The Prophet:** already in the other world. A false miracle-worker whose 'miracles' are demonic, a dark mirror of Aveline's saints. Theme: true faith against counterfeit faith.
- **Bound souls (unique to these two, not a rule of the world):** their souls are connected. The reveal is that they've been working together all along. Through the Lord, the Prophet knows the real world and the war, and he is trying to break through the veil and take it over. The cult's tear in the opening is step one of his invasion.

## Villain names (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Guérin de Montclair:** the Lord ('clair', bright, is ironic).
- **Simeon the Lamp:** the Prophet, a false light.
- **Sulthrane:** the demon (an invented name) who gave Simeon his powers, motivations and goals. Simeon is his servant. Sulthrane is the final boss.

## Act structure (DRAFT 2026-11-09, not locked)
1. **The Tear:** an ordinary war at a frontier castle; the cult's rite tears the veil. Tristan sees the Satanist killed by a Fiend, joins Dismas's hunters, and answers Aveline's call at the holy stronghold, where the first true Demon is attacking. Ends there, with the map game (consecrating nodes) beginning.
2. **The Lamp:** Aveline tells Tristan that she, Godric and Lucien saw him fall into the tear and followed. Lucien was taken in a later battle; Godric went after him and she has heard from neither since. Simeon is the opponent from the first (never an ally). The party takes jobs from a good-guy force Aveline already joined (identity TBD). Quests lead into Simeon's forces. Climax: they find Godric facing Vaskar and team up against him. (Whether Vaskar is defeated or escapes, and whether Lucien is present or only reported, is still open.)
3. **Two Faces:** a way into or a glimpse of the real world shows Guérin opening more tears. Reveal: bound souls, working together to break through for Sulthrane. Lucien doubts. Low point: a costly defeat; Tristan's faith holds.
4. **Holy Ground:** Lucien returns (the prodigal son). Holy ground spreads. Guérin and Simeon are beaten on both sides of the veil at once. Sulthrane is defeated with saints and angels. They return home and the veil is sealed.

## Act 1 opening (LOCKED 2026-11-09)
- **The mission:** the squad (Tristan, Aveline, Godric, Lucien) is part of a Templar detachment sent to Guérin de Montclair's castle after a call for help about Satanists.
- **The call for help:** a frightened castle chaplain wrote to the Templars behind Guérin's back. When the squad arrives, the chaplain has "gone to visit his sister." His body is later found in the cellars.
- **Guérin as gracious host:** he is warm, charming and dismissive ("Rumours, brothers. Peasants see devils in every shadow. But you're welcome to look, after supper."), and he quietly steers them away from the lower keep. Players should read it as pride or negligence, not villainy, so the Act 3 reveal still lands.
- **Pushing through:** a servant slips the squad a warning, and they inspect anyway.
- **Lucien's seed:** he admires Guérin and argues that they should respect the baron's word. Tristan overrules him. This foreshadows Lucien's turn.
- **The fight:** the descent into the cellars is the tutorial battle against human cultists, and it looks like an ordinary war fight. Then the ritual completes, the veil tears, and Tristan is pulled through. Aveline, Godric and Lucien see him fall and follow, and they are later separated.
- **After the tear (FFTA2-style, LOCKED 2026-11-09):** Tristan lands with a Satanist, a Fiend kills the Satanist, and then a hunting party from this world rescues Tristan. Its leader is a hero character, a converted former cultist who once served the profane side and now hunts it. He's living proof that someone can come back, which mirrors and foreshadows Lucien's return (name: **Dismas**, after the penitent thief). Backstory: he is a native of the other world who once served the profane side alongside his closest friend, a sworn brother in spirit but not by blood. When Dismas turned to the light he had to "betray" that friend. The friend stayed in the dark and rose to become Simeon's lieutenant. He is the one who recruits Lucien, using the same "they'll never value you" pitch he once used on Dismas, and he is so far gone into Sulthrane's power that he is barely a man any more. Dismas can't save him, only end him. His name is **Vaskar**. This mirrors Lucien in reverse: Lucien is the brother who comes back, and Dismas's friend is the one who doesn't. Tristan joins Dismas's hunting party, which becomes the first team. Aveline, Godric and Lucien are found later as planned.

- **Who is from the real world:** only the four heroes (Tristan, Aveline, Godric, Lucien) and Guérin and his cult come from our world (confirmed). Dismas, his hunting party and everyone else native to the other world come from there.

## Act 1 beats after the tear (LOCKED 2026-11-09)
1. **First fight and rescue:** the Fiend that killed the Satanist turns on Tristan and summons little minions (Imps or Wretches). Dismas's hunting party arrives, and Tristan fights alongside them. This is the first battle with the hunters, and he proves he's no pushover even though he's never seen such creatures. Dismas gives him a crash course and is himself a little puzzled by how confused Tristan is.
2. **Joining the hunters:** after the Fiend falls, Tristan joins the party for a few minor quests that teach how this world works. Along the way Tristan explains where he's from, and Dismas ponders it.
3. **Act 1 finale, the quest "Saint-Caller Calling for Aid":** Aveline is at the holy stronghold calling for help because a true Demon is attacking the place. The party answers, and meeting her is the final encounter of Act 1. This is the first true Demon the player faces, a step up from the opening Fiend, and in the fight she calls on blessed help as a Saint-Caller. The act ends at the stronghold, where the map game begins. (The Demon's name is still TBD.)
- **Vaskar:** saved for Act 2, with no Act 1 glimpse.
- **How the others crossed (LOCKED 2026-11-09):** Aveline, Godric and Lucien saw Tristan fall into the tear and followed him through. They were later separated, as Aveline recounts at the start of Act 2.
- **Saints in this world:** blessed help that certain individuals (Saint-Callers) can call on.
- **Bestiary words (LOCKED 2026-11-09):** these are used as kinds (and names) of enemies, not as competing labels for the same thing:
  - **Imps, Wretches, Blights, Hollows (or the Hollowed), Gheists, the Fallen:** distinct lesser kinds the player will face.
  - **Fiends:** mid-tier. The creature that kills the Satanist in the Act 1 opening is a Fiend (with lesser spawn), so Tristan's first fight is against a Fiend, not a true Demon.
  - **Demons:** reserved for stronger and named enemies. The first true Demon appears in the Act 1 finale, attacking the holy stronghold where Aveline is calling for aid. Facing one is a big deal after the opening Fiend.
  - **Sulthrane** sits above them all.

## Act 2 beats (DRAFT 2026-11-09, revised from Cletus's notes, awaiting a few calls)
- **Bridge from Act 1:** after finding Aveline, Act 2 begins with her explaining that she, Godric and Lucien saw Tristan fall into the tear and all three followed. This is how Tristan learns the other two are in this world as well.
- **What happened to them:** they fought in a battle, and Lucien was taken. Godric went after him. Aveline has not heard from either since.
- **Simeon:** always seen as the opponent, never as a false ally. His following is still a counterfeit faith the world must contend with, but the heroes do not trust him.
- **The good-guy force (identity TBD):** a force in this world already opposing Simeon. Aveline (and the others, before they were separated) joined it. Tristan's clan then becomes part of it, or at least takes jobs and quests from it. Options pitched: a native holy order that posts jobs (recommended); a loose coalition of stronghold, hunters and free towns; or the Wardens, an army sworn to consecrate ground. Name ideas: the Vigil, the Wardens, the Order of the Sepulchre, the Hallowed Host.
- **The act:** more quests and missions, which eventually lead to facing more of Simeon's forces.
- **Climax:** the party runs into Godric facing Vaskar and teams up with him against Vaskar. Open: whether Vaskar falls or escapes (recommendation: he escapes, wounded), and whether Lucien is at the fight or Godric only brings news that Lucien has joined the enemy (recommendation: Godric saw Lucien at Vaskar's side and Lucien would not come, so Aveline's break can happen here).
