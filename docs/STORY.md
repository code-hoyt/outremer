# Outremer: story bible

The working doc for story decisions, written with Cletus. Earlier test characters (Renaud, Yusuf, Wren) are set aside; the story starts from scratch (2026-11-09).

## Core (locked 2026-11-09)
- **What the player should feel at the end:** satisfied and happy. The heroes win, and the win is earned.
- **Moral frame:** the Christian knights are the good guys. Show it through their deeds (keeping vows when it costs them, protecting the weak, mercy, staying true) rather than telling it.
- **Tone:** a clear, triumphant, heroic story, closer to classic Final Fantasy or Tolkien than to something grim and grey.

## Antagonists (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Both:** a human antagonist early on who turns out to serve a darker power.
- **Demons** are real and play a part.
- **Saints and angels** are on the side of the light, possibly as summons in combat.

## How real the myth is (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Open from the start:** the supernatural is an accepted part of the world (relics, blessings, known legends).
- **Escalation:** the war first looks like a normal one, but strange events build until the true, demonic scale of the threat is revealed.

## Setting (locked 2026-11-09)
- **Lightly fictionalised Crusades:** our own characters and smaller towns and castles in the real Crusader states. Real kings and campaigns are heard about in the background, but the story is our own.
- **Two layers:** the real world, and a spiritual side of the same world where the true (demonic) enemies are seen. The story starts in the real world and crosses over as it escalates.
- **Gameplay idea:** a map's spiritual version could have different terrain, demons in place of soldiers, and saints you can call on.

## Crossing over (locked 2026-11-09)
- **The tear:** a Satanist cult tears the veil, and it pulls the heroes into the other world early in Act 1 (like the book in FFTA).
- **Sceptical protagonist:** he doesn't really believe in the supernatural, then lands in a world where it's everyday and is almost in shock. His arc runs from scepticism to conviction.

## Mechanic idea: holy and profane ground (leaning, 2026-11-09)
- Territory you hold is holy, and enemy territory is profane.
- Map nodes are worth taking for what they offer (shops, mercenaries, upgrades, saint summons). Winning a node consecrates it; profane nodes favour demons.
- No extra button press; it comes out of which battles you choose. Different node priorities make each playthrough different.

## Opening and the scattered squad (locked 2026-11-09)
- **The squad is scattered** when the veil tears. Finding each other is an early goal.
- **Opening scene:** the protagonist is pulled through alongside one of the Satanists. Both arrive confused. A demon finds them, the Satanist worships it or panics, and it kills him. That's the hero's crash course in the new world.
- **His squad (about 3, found later in different places):**
  - a loyal friend;
  - a love interest;
  - someone who switches sides (direction to be decided; suggestion: starts on the wrong side and earns redemption).
